Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Billio helps you keep track of subscriptions you pay for by card, so a charge never surprises you. This page explains what Billio collects, how it uses it, and the choices you have.

What Billio stores

  • Your account: your name and email address.
  • What you enter: your subscriptions (name, amount, currency, how often, next charge date), card nicknames (name, currency and optionally the last 4 digits), and your settings such as exchange rates.
  • Billio never asks for or stores your full card number, PIN or CVV.

Gmail access (optional)

If you choose "Find subscriptions in Gmail", Billio asks Google for read-only access to your mailbox (the gmail.readonly permission). It is used for one thing only: finding receipts and renewal emails so Billio can suggest subscriptions for you to add.

  • Your emails are read in your own browser, on your device. Email content is not sent to or stored on Billio's servers.
  • The access Google gives Billio is kept only in your browser's memory while the scan runs. It is not saved.
  • Billio saves only the subscriptions you tick and choose to add, such as the service name, amount and date.
  • No person at Billio reads your emails. Billio does not sell, share or use Gmail data for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Billio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You can remove Billio's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Where your data lives

Your account and the subscriptions you enter are stored with our database provider, Supabase, and protected so that only you can read your own records. Reminder emails are sent through Resend to the email address on your account.

Reminder emails

Billio emails you a few days before a charge you are tracking, to your account email. We do not send marketing emails.

Deleting your data

In Billio, open Settings and choose "Delete all my data" to remove your subscriptions and cards. To delete your account completely, contact us at the address below and we will remove it.
